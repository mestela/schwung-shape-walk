# Shape Walk v0.7.0

First public release of Shape Walk for Schwung on Ableton Move.

- **Shape Walk MIDI FX** generates repeatable chord sequences by moving fourth and fifth dyads around Move's chromatic grid. Play steps from pads or sync them to the clock.
- **Shape Walk Pads** puts generated chords on the left 4×4 and saved, playable chords on the right 4×4. Copy, edit, transpose, and record the saved chords into Move.
- Shape the results with spacing, smoothness, bass motion, inversion, and tonal colours from open through major, minor, sevenths, and ninths.
- A seed recreates the same chords with the same settings. Saved pads remain independent of later generator changes.

## Install

Download `shape-walk-v0.7.0.tar.gz` and extract it into `/data/UserData/schwung/modules/` on Move. Restart Schwung. The archive includes both modules and leaves saved chord data untouched.

Requires [Schwung](https://github.com/mestela/schwung) on Ableton Move. See the [README](https://github.com/mestela/schwung-shape-walk#readme) for controls and build instructions.
